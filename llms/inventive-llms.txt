# Inventive AI

> Inventive AI is an AI-powered platform that automates responses to RFPs, RFIs, DDQs, and security questionnaires, helping proposal and sales teams respond up to 90% faster with higher win rates. It unifies knowledge from Google Drive, SharePoint, Notion, and Confluence into a single searchable hub and generates context-aware first drafts grounded in verified content. Inventive is a Y Combinator company backed by Sierra Ventures and Wing Venture Capital. It is delivered as a closed SaaS application and does not currently publish a public developer API, SDKs, or developer documentation.

## Product

- [Inventive AI home](https://www.inventive.ai/): RFP/RFI/DDQ/security-questionnaire response automation.
- [Features](https://www.inventive.ai/features): AI RFP responses, unified knowledge hub, AI content manager, AI agents.
- [RFP platform](https://www.inventive.ai/solution/rfp-platform): End-to-end proposal response workflow.
